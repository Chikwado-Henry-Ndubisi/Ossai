App Rules & How It Works

1. Welcome to ossai75.ng

This app is made for pregnant mothers in Nigeria. We help you track your pregnancy, give you health tips, and reward you for completing daily care tasks.

2. How It Works

• Create free account with your real name and email.
• Login daily to see your tasks.
• Complete tasks like reading health tips, drinking water, exercise, clinic visit.
• For each task you complete, you earn MAMACOIN and BABYCOIN.
• Use coins for bonuses, gifts, and special Nigeria offers.

3. Rules You Must Abide

• Use your real information. No fake account.
• One account per mother.
• Do not spam or cheat tasks. System will detect and ban.
• Respect other mothers in community.
• This app does not replace your doctor. Always visit hospital for medical care.
• Your data is safe and will not be shared without permission.

4. Rewards

MAMACOIN and BABYCOIN are reward points inside the app. They have no cash value but give you access to special care packages, health info, and bonuses.

5. Agreement

By clicking AGREE below, you agree to follow all rules and use the app properly for your pregnancy care.
