1. ABOUT OSSAI75.NG

ossai75.ng is a pregnancy care support platform for Nigerian mothers. We provide health education, daily care tasks, community support, and reward system. We are NOT a hospital. Always visit qualified doctor for medical advice.

2. OUR 5 COIN REWARD SYSTEM (EXPLAINED)

a) MAMACOIN: Main reward for daily mama care tasks - reading tips, water, rest, clinic visit. Earn up to 10 daily.

b) BABYCOIN: Reward for baby care knowledge - baby nutrition, vaccination reminders, baby growth tracking.

c) QUARTERCOIN: Quarterly bonus coin. Complete 90 days consistent tasks without missing 7 days straight, you earn Quartercoin. Used for special quarterly gift box.

d) GROWTHCOIN: Long-term growth coin. For mothers who stay active from pregnancy to delivery tracking. Very valuable. Redeemable for bigger support packages after 6 months activity.
